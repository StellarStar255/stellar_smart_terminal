终端右键菜单中的「快速命令」和「本地快速命令」现在会限制长列表的高度。用触控板双指滚动、滚轮或拖动滚动条时，列表在固定的子菜单内滚动，可以一直滚到底。较长的命令名称单行显示，悬停时可查看全名。

Long **Quick Commands** and **Local Quick Commands** lists now scroll inside a bounded terminal context submenu. Use a two-finger gesture, mouse wheel, or scrollbar to reach the last command without shifting the popup. Long names stay on one line, with the full name available on hover.

Use `*-windows-x64-setup.exe` on Windows, `*-macOS-arm64.dmg` on Apple Silicon, or `*-linux-amd64.deb` on Debian or Ubuntu. You can also check for updates in the app.
