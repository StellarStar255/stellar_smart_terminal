## Codex 跨页拖选

修复了跨页拖选时一松开鼠标蓝色高亮就消失的问题。即使最后一次滚动在松手后才显示，仍在屏幕上的已选正文也会保持高亮。复制保留松手时的选区，包括已滚出窗口的内容。

## Multi-page selection in Codex

Selected text stays highlighted when a pending scroll moves the transcript after you release the mouse. Copying retains the selection you finished, including text that has scrolled out of view.
