## Terminal tabs

The gray output dot, orange attention dot and green completion dot now share a fixed position. Tab titles and close buttons stay in place when a dot appears, changes color or clears. Selecting a tab highlights its top edge and title color while keeping its font weight consistent.

## 简体中文

后台输出的灰点、等待确认的橙点和完成时的绿点现在显示在同一位置。状态点出现、变色或消失时，标签标题和关闭按钮保持原位。切换标签时，顶边和文字颜色标出当前标签，长标题也保持原来的字重。
